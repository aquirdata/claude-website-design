1. The Aquire Security Mandate: Zero-Trust Engineering

Before outlining standard website data collection, it is vital to establish our data operational framework for enterprise clients and partners:

  • Read-Only Metadata Infrastructure: Our proprietary 48-Hour Data Health Scorecard pipeline operates strictly on a read-only metadata level. We analyze cloud asset configurations, log metadata, schema structures, and telemetry signals across environments like AWS, GCP, Snowflake, or Databricks.
  • No Core Data Movement: Aquire does not copy, harvest, ingest, or store the underlying production, transactional, customer, or proprietary databases belonging to your enterprise or your referred clients.
  • Non-Invasive Verification: Our diagnostic agents are deployed via zero-trust protocols, ensuring our engineering evaluation cannot manipulate, modify, or retain sensitive corporate assets.

4. How Information is Shared and Disclosed

Aquire does not sell, rent, or lease your information to marketers or third-party brokers. We disclose information only under the following strict conditions:

  • To Third-Party Service Handlers: We share operational form data securely with internal cloud productivity environments (such as Google Workspace via automated secure text scripts) strictly to log inbound business pipelines and dispatch notification alerts.
  • Legal and Regulatory Compliance: We will disclose information if required to do so by applicable law, subpoena, or governmental mandate to ensure regulatory corporate safety.

5. Global Compliance & Data Protection Rights

Depending on your geographic location or corporate operational theatre, you may hold specific rights under frameworks such as the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and other evolving federal data mandates.

Aquire fully respects and facilitates these data rights, including:

  • The Right to Access / Transparency: You may request a clean summary of the personal data we hold regarding your professional profile or corporate firm.
  • The Right to Deletion ("Right to be Forgotten"): You may request that Aquire erase your partner registration, contact records, or historical leads from our internal log sheets.
  • The Right to Rectification: You may correct or amend missing or outdated profile fields at any time.

To exercise any of these privacy rights, please contact our data governance team directly at legal@aquiredata.com.

6. Information Security Architecture

We enforce industry-standard security baselines to shield data from unauthorized access, loss, or manipulation. Form submittals to aquiredata.com/partners pass through encrypted HTTPS channels, and internal administrative logs are isolated within credentialed pipelines.

While we apply vigilant, high-velocity defenses to safeguard data, no digital transfer layer or cloud architecture can be guaranteed 100% immune to network vectors. We urge all users to access portal endpoints using secure corporate networks.
